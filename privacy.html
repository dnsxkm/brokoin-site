알림을 켜면 다음 세 가지가 저장됩니다.

접속 시각 최초 등록 시각, 마지막 접속 시각 사용하지 않는 기기를 자동 삭제하기 위해

수집하지 않는 것

이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 생년월일, 성별, 주소, 위치 정보, 연락처, 사진, 광고 식별자(IDFA), 기기 모델, 통신사 정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 앱 사용 행태를 추적하는 분석 도구를 사용하지 않으며, 제3자 광고 SDK를 포함하지 않습니다.

계정 기능이 없어 이용자를 개인으로 식별하지 않습니다. 저장되는 푸시 토큰은 기기에 발급된 값이며 특정 개인과 연결되지 않습니다.

2. 이용 목적

수집한 정보는 알림 발송이라는 단일 목적으로만 사용합니다. 마케팅, 프로파일링, 자동화된 의사결정, 제3자 판매에 사용하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

코인레벨 (Coin Level) has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number. If you do not enable notifications, we collect nothing at all — all market data, charts, and news features work without any data collection.

What we collect

Only when you turn notifications on, we store three things:

  • Push token — an ExponentPushToken[...] string issued to your device, used solely to deliver notifications
  • Notification preferences — which alert types, symbols, and minimum amounts you selected
  • Timestamps — first registration and last seen, used to purge unused devices

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device models. We use no analytics SDK and no third-party advertising SDK. Because there are no accounts, we do not identify you as an individual.

How it is used and shared

The data is used for one purpose: delivering the notifications you asked for. It is never used for marketing, profiling, or sold to anyone. Processing is carried out by Expo (push delivery, United States), Apple APNs (delivery, United States and elsewhere), and Railway (server hosting, Singapore).

Retention and deletion

Turning notifications off in the app deletes the record from our server immediately. Deleting the app has the same effect — tokens that fail delivery are pruned automatically. Devices inactive for 90 days are deleted automatically. We keep no backups of this data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing. The fastest route is to turn notifications off or delete the app, which erases the record right away. For anything else, contact dongskim01@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Data sources

Market, liquidation, and open interest data come from Binance's public API. The Fear & Greed Index comes from Alternative.me. News headlines, summaries, and links come from publishers' RSS feeds and are always shown with the publisher's name. No user information is transmitted to these sources.
